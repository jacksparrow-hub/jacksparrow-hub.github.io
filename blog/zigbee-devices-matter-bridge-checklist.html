Can a Zigbee device work in a Matter home?

Yes, sometimes, through a compatible Matter bridge. A Zigbee bulb does not become a native Matter-over-Thread bulb just because your home has a Thread border router. The bridge translates between the device's original network and a Matter controller. Whether an exact model is exposed and which features carry across depends on the manufacturer's bridge implementation and your platform. This is a research checklist, not a tested product recommendation.

What the bridge changes, and what it does not

PartIts jobCommon misconception
Zigbee or Z-Wave end deviceCommunicates on its own existing network.It does not speak native Matter merely because it appears in a Matter app.
Matter bridgeExposes supported non-Matter devices as virtual Matter devices to the Matter side.Not every older hub has Matter bridge support; check model and firmware.
Matter controllerManages the exposed device in a Matter platform.The app may show only supported functions.
Thread border routerConnects Thread devices to the home IP network.It is not automatically a Zigbee bridge, even though both Thread and Zigbee use related radio technology.

The Connectivity Standards Alliance's bridging explainer gives the virtual-device and mixed-light examples; its Matter FAQ says there is no native interoperability between Zigbee and Matter without a bridge.

A model-level checklist before buying a bridge

  1. Write down your existing devices. Exact manufacturer, model number, current hub, and firmware. A seller's "Zigbee compatible" badge alone does not identify what will be exposed through Matter.
  2. Find the manufacturer's Matter bridge documentation. Confirm that your hub model and firmware support a Matter bridge, not just a future announcement or a separate product line. Ask which linked device types and functions are exported.
  3. Check the controller platform. Google's supported device-type page says devices behind a Matter bridge appear as normal devices in Google Home, while the bridge itself is an inert Control Bridge there. That does not guarantee every advanced device feature appears in the app.
  4. Check the bridge's network and setup requirements. See the maker's current manual, your platform's hub requirements and the Matter hub versus Thread router guide. Do not buy a Thread router as a substitute for a maker's Zigbee bridge.
  5. Test one device before migrating the whole home. In your own setup, confirm the crucial controls and automations work in the intended app. Document what does not carry over before relying on it.

Example: mixed lighting

Imagine one native Matter light and one existing Zigbee light linked to a maker's Matter-capable bridge. The Alliance's example explains that a Matter controller can issue a light command to both: the native light receives Matter directly and the bridge translates the command to Zigbee for the older light. This illustrates the architecture, not a promise that every bulb, dimmer function or bridge model works alike. Check your maker's export list and your app's controls.
