How to share a Matter device across smart-home apps

Matter's multi-admin feature lets a compatible device be controlled from more than one platform. Setting it up is different from signing a second phone into the same account: the existing app opens a sharing flow and the second app adds the device. This guide follows Google's published Google Home-first flow; check the other platform's instructions too. We have not tested a household setup.

Before you share: three separate checks

CheckWhy it matters
Confirm it is a Matter-enabled device already added to Google Home.Google's multi-admin steps start with an already set-up Matter device, not a generic Works with Google Home integration.
Confirm a compatible Matter hub for each platform.Google says each Matter-certified app needs its own compatible hub. Check the second platform's own hub list rather than assuming the Google hub serves both apps.
Check network and device type.Thread devices need a suitable Thread border router, and features shown in one app may not match another. Read our hub guide and model-check worksheet.

Google Home-first sharing flow

  1. Open the device in Google Home. Touch and hold its tile, then open More or Settings and Linked Matter apps & services.
  2. Tap Link apps & services. Choose the second Matter-certified platform app from the list. Google says that app must be installed before it appears as a choice.
  3. Let Google Home open the second app and follow that app's add-device steps. Google's alternative is Use pairing code or Share with QR code to generate a temporary code; the QR method calls for another mobile device with the second platform app installed.
  4. Confirm the device appears in both apps. Try the particular control you need in the second app, not just the device's presence.

These steps track Google's current setup and multi-admin help. App menu names can change. If the second app is not listed, first check whether it is installed and supports Matter, then whether its own required hub is present. Do not expose a temporary pairing code publicly or reuse an old QR image as a permanent setup link.

What multi-admin does not promise

Sharing a device does not make every manufacturer's advanced function available in each app. Google's Matter device-type table itself distinguishes Google Home app, voice and smart-display controls. For a vital feature, consult the other platform's current documentation and test it in your own setup. An original device QR code and a temporary multi-admin pairing code serve different setup flows; use the sharing option from the already connected app for a second platform.

Source and scope

This is documentation-based guidance for a Google Home-first setup, not a cross-platform compatibility test or endorsement. Sources: Google Home Matter setup and sharing, Connectivity Standards Alliance Matter FAQ, Google Home hub preparation, and Google device-type support.
