About Little Star Stories

Little Star Stories is a small, free library of original picture books for children aged 2 to 7. The stories are gentle, short and made for bedtime: kindness, sharing, courage and friendship, told with three friends from the night sky and the sea.

Every story, character and picture here is original. There is nothing to buy and nothing to sign up for. If you enjoy the books, the best thanks is sharing this page with another family.

Written and made with love by Jack Sparrow.
